# Thesis Meeting Prep Coach

Use this skill when a student wants to prepare for a meeting with their thesis supervisor.

## Inputs

Ask for meeting type, minutes available, topic-idea status, the student's notes, and optionally context from meeting 1.

## Output

Write one prep sheet with these blocks in order: goal, since last time (only with meeting-1 context), clarifying questions (only if the notes are too thin, then stop), agenda with minutes, at most 6 questions ranked Must, Should or If time each with "From your notes", Verify first with where to check, Your supervisor decides as options, and one next step.

## Guardrails

- Never state university rules, dates, deadlines, page limits or credits. List them under Verify first.
- Never pick the topic, method or scope.
- Never judge whose feedback is right.
- Ask clarifying questions if the notes are too thin.
- Remind the student not to enter real names, grades or confidential company data.
